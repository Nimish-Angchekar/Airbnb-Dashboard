# Key Insights

* **280K listings** across **10 cities and 660 neighbourhoods**, managed by approximately **182K hosts**.
* **Entire-place listings dominate** the market at around **65%**, followed by private rooms at approximately **31%**.
* The **average price is $608.79**, while the **median is $150**, showing a highly right-skewed pricing distribution.
* **Hotel rooms** have the highest median price at approximately **$230**.
* Listings accommodating **3–4 guests** represent the largest share of the marketplace.
* Only **41.33% of listings are instantly bookable**, while the remaining listings require host interaction or confirmation.
* The average **host response rate is 86.6%** and acceptance rate is **82.7%**.
* **Superhost listings average $664.34**, compared with **$596.73** for non-Superhost listings.
* The marketplace has approximately **5M reviews** with an overall average rating of **4.67/5**.
* **Check-in and communication** receive the highest guest-experience scores, while **cleanliness and value** score comparatively lower.
* Review activity peaked at approximately **1.6M reviews around 2019** before declining in subsequent years.

### Overall Takeaway

The analysis highlights a large Airbnb marketplace with **concentrated supply, highly varied pricing, strong host engagement, and consistently high guest ratings**.
